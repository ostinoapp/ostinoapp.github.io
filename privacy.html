Privacy Policy - Ostino

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Ostino is a piano practice app for iPhone and iPad. It opens MIDI files and shows them as falling notes on a keyboard and as sheet music. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean the developer of Ostino.

We are based in Poland, and we handle personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In practice there is very little to handle, because Ostino doesn't collect personal data at all.

2. In Short

Ostino runs entirely on your device and does not collect any data about you:

  • No account: You don't sign up or sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address, or any other details.
  • No internet connection: The app doesn't connect to the internet. Nothing you do in Ostino is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No tracking, ads, or analytics: Ostino contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your files stay with you: The MIDI files you import are stored on your device, and we have no way to see them.

3. What Ostino Stores on Your Device

Ostino keeps the following information in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad. None of it leaves your device, and none of it is available to us.

3.1 MIDI Files You Import

You can add songs with the + button, which opens the standard iOS file picker, or open a MIDI file in Ostino from the Files app, Mail, or AirDrop. Ostino saves a copy of the file so the song appears in your Library. The app can only open files you choose. It cannot browse the rest of your files, photos, or media library.

3.2 Settings

Ostino remembers your preferences, such as falling notes or sheet music view, note names, metronome, count-in, sustain, and whether the MIDI keyboard is switched on, so they're the same the next time you open the app.

3.3 MIDI Keyboard

If you switch on MIDI Keyboard and connect a MIDI keyboard or digital piano, Ostino reads which keys you press so it can light them up on screen. This note input is used only while you play. It is not recorded or saved, and Ostino sends nothing to the keyboard.

3.4 Device Backups

If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Apple includes Ostino's songs and settings in that backup, as it does for other apps. Apple's privacy policy covers those backups.

3.5 Deleting Your Data

To remove a song, swipe left on it in the Library and tap Delete. To remove everything Ostino has stored, delete the app from your device.

4. Information Handled by Apple

Apple provides some services around the app under its own privacy policy, which you can read at apple.com/legal/privacy:

  • App Store: Apple handles downloads and updates of Ostino. We don't receive your Apple Account details or any payment information.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and general usage statistics. Apple aggregates or anonymizes this data, so it does not identify you. We use it only to find and fix bugs. You can turn it off at any time in the same settings.

Ostino does not use any other third-party services. We do not sell, rent, or share data about you with anyone.

5. When You Email Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply and help you. We keep the conversation only as long as we need it to resolve your request, and we'll delete it sooner if you ask.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

The app itself processes no personal data. The only personal data we may receive is the email correspondence described in Section 5. We process it on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering your questions and supporting the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also restrict or object to its processing and ask to receive a copy of it. Because Ostino collects no data, these rights apply in practice only to emails you have sent us. To use any of them, write to the address below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Poland, that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO). You can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live.

8. Children's Privacy

Ostino does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Children can use the app without sharing any information about themselves. If a child has emailed us and a parent or guardian would like that correspondence deleted, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of Ostino changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released. If a change means the app starts collecting data, we will describe it here first.
